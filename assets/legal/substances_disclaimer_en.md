# Before you begin

The information in this section is **reference material**, not medical advice. It does not
replace consultation with a doctor or pharmacist.

Data completeness varies by substance — check the status badge ("complete" / "partial" /
"chemistry only"). Missing fields are shown as "not available", never guessed or filled in.

Always discuss dosage or interaction questions with a doctor or pharmacist before changing what
you take.

**In an emergency:** 112 (EU) · 911 (US) · 999 (UK)

shown on the relevant substance's detail view.
